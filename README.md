# Market & Customer Segmentation Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my Market & Customer Segmentation Analytics Portfolio! This repository showcases data-driven segmentation projects that apply unsupervised machine learning and customer-value analysis to uncover business insights, operational patterns, and customer behavior.

---

## 📂 Portfolio Projects

### 1. [Top 100 US Independent Restaurants Clustering](./us_independent_restaurants_kmeans)

- **Method**: K-Means Clustering
- **Domain**: Hospitality & Food Industry
- **Objective**: Segment the highest-grossing independent US restaurants based on revenue, average check size, and guest volume to identify distinct operational business models.
- **Tech Stack**: Python, `scikit-learn`, `pandas`
- **Key Skills**: Feature scaling, cluster evaluation, business persona profiling, and strategic recommendations.

### 2. [UK Online Retail RFM Customer Segmentation](./uk_online_retail_rfm_segmentation)

- **Method**: Quartile-Based RFM Scoring
- **Domain**: E-Commerce & Retail
- **Objective**: Segment UK retail customers by purchase recency, frequency, and monetary value to identify high-value customers and support retention and targeted marketing strategies.
- **Dataset**: UCI Machine Learning Repository — Online Retail
- **Tech Stack**: Python, `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `openpyxl`
- **Key Skills**: Transactional data cleaning, customer-level aggregation, RFM feature engineering, customer-value segmentation, and data visualization.
