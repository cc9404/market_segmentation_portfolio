# Market & Customer Segmentation Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my Market & Customer Segmentation Portfolio! This repository showcases data-driven segmentation projects applying unsupervised machine learning techniques to uncover business insights and customer behavior.

---

## 📂 Portfolio Projects

### 1. [Top 100 US Independent Restaurants Clustering](./us_independent_restaurants_kmeans)
* **Algorithm**: K-Means Clustering
* **Domain**: Hospitality & Food Industry
* **Objective**: Segment the highest-grossing independent US restaurants based on revenue, average check size, and guest volume to identify distinct operational business models.
* **Tech Stack**: Python, `scikit-learn`, `pandas`, `seaborn`
